Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Do I need to buy extra hardware or camera lenses?
No! JoJo Cam uses the high-quality camera already built into your spare smartphone or tablet.
❓ Does JoJo Cam work if the internet goes down?
Yes! As long as both devices are connected to the same local Wi-Fi router, JoJo Cam streams peer-to-peer even if your internet connection is offline.
❓ Is my baby video feed safe from hackers?
100% yes. Because there are no cloud servers storing or relaying your stream, outside hackers cannot access your feed. Encryption keys are generated locally between your paired phones.
❓ Can I control the camera remotely?
Yes! From your viewer phone, you can remotely zoom in/out, turn on the flashlight gently, and switch between front and back cameras.
