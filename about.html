About JoJo Cam

Empowering parents with private, cloud-free nursery monitoring

Our Mission: Peace of Mind for Parents

JoJo Cam was created to solve a simple problem faced by millions of working parents: How can I keep an eye on my sleeping baby in another room without spending hundreds on hardware or risking cloud privacy leaks?

Most commercial smart baby monitors upload live video feeds to central cloud servers. We believe your baby's nursery video should remain strictly private inside your home.

Why Peer-to-Peer (P2P)?

JoJo Cam connects your devices directly over your home Wi-Fi network using standard end-to-end encryption (AES-256-GCM). By eliminating cloud servers entirely:

  • No External Server Uploads: Video feeds travel directly between your paired phones over your router.
  • Zero Subscription Fees: No monthly cloud storage or paywalls.
  • $0 Hardware Cost: Repurpose an old iPhone, iPad, or Android phone sitting in your drawer.
  • Complete Offline Operation: Streams seamlessly even if your internet connection is down.

Built for Caregiver Freedom

Whether you are attending work meetings, cooking dinner, or taking a much-needed break, JoJo Cam gives you the confidence to focus on your task while staying connected to your baby's crib.

Get Started Today

Download JoJo Cam from the App Store or Google Play Store and experience instant, private baby monitoring in under 30 seconds.
