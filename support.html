Quick Troubleshooting Tips

📶 Devices won't connect / pairing failed?
Make sure both phones are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network (2.4GHz or 5GHz). Ensure "Local Network" permission is granted in your iOS Settings under JoJo Cam.
📷 QR Code scanning issues?
Ensure the camera lens on your scanning phone is clean and bright. Move your phone closer to or further from the QR code until it focuses.
🔋 Keeping the nursery camera running long-term?
Keep the spare phone plugged into a charger near the crib. Turn down screen brightness on the camera device to prevent excess heat.
